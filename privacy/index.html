Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Vebro Solutions, the registered brand operating vebro.net and the Vebro Solutions assistant alerts SMS program, collects and uses information.

Who receives messages

Vebro Solutions assistant texts are sent only to the business owner, who opts in by texting START from his own phone.

Information we collect

  • Mobile phone number – the number that receives or sends text messages to the program.
  • Message content – the text of messages sent to and from the program.

How we use information

We use your mobile number and message content only to send the owner’s assistant alerts and replies, and to respond to your messages, including HELP and STOP requests. We do not use this information for marketing.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Information is shared only with the messaging and carrier providers needed to deliver text messages, or when required by law.

SMS messaging

  • Opt out: reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. You will receive one confirmation message and no further messages unless you opt in again.
  • Help: reply HELP for help, or contact us using the details below.
  • Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

See our Terms and Conditions for full SMS program terms.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.
