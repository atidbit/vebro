The idea

About

VB’s Bar + Grill is a concept for a neighborhood room that takes the grill seriously and the bar just as seriously. The draft idea is simple: hardwood fire, generous plates, and a place you could make a regular stop.

This page is a design study, not a working restaurant. The story, menu, hours, and location below are placeholder copy so the site can be reviewed as if it had already launched.
