A children’s picture book

It’s Safe to Ask

A gentle story about asking questions, speaking up, and respecting each other.

Vernon S. Brown, Ph.D., Ed.D.

Coming soon

8.5×8.5 in full-color picture book, 32 pages.

In Ms. June’s class, Pip has a question he almost does not ask: “Do the seeds need room to grow?” Ms. June bends down and listens. She does not laugh. Soon his friends speak up too. And when they do not agree, they are still kind.

For adults: The Supervisor Effect

About the author

Vernon S. Brown, Ph.D., Ed.D., is a leadership and organizational psychologist and a professor of applied behavioral sciences. He studies psychological safety: whether people feel safe to speak up, ask questions, and share ideas at work.
