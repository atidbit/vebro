About Dr. Brown

Vernon Brown, Ph.D., Ed.D., is a leadership and organizational psychologist specializing in psychological safety, leadership, and organizational climate, and Professor of Applied Behavioral Sciences at The University of Arizona Global Campus. A U.S. Navy veteran with more than three decades of government service, including twenty-two years on active duty as a Sailor and Navy Chief, he studies psychological safety among federal employees at both the employee and supervisor levels.

He is the author of the forthcoming book The Supervisor Effect and founded Vebro Solutions to turn that research into practical tools for supervisors, leaders, educators, and researchers.
